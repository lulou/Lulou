Create a premium, one-time guidance system called Lulou Guide.

Goal:
Users must naturally understand how Lulou works without feeling like they’re being taught or interrupted. The experience should feel prestigious, elegant, and almost like a private matchmaker quietly guiding them.

Design inspiration:
Apple × Raya × Aman Resort × Luxury Concierge.

Absolutely avoid:

Cheap tutorials
Coach marks everywhere
Cartoon speech bubbles
Confetti
“Great job!”
“Congratulations!”
Duolingo-style onboarding
Popups that block actions
General rules:

Each guide appears only once.
Persist completion per user.
Guides can be replayed from Settings → “Lulou Guide”.
Elegant glass cards with subtle blur.
Serif titles.
Soft fade animations.
Respect reduced motion settings.
Auto dismiss after 3–5 seconds.
Optional “Got it” button.
Never interrupt typing, calls, or swiping.
Safe area support for iPhone.
Premium haptics only.
Create a table:

guide_key
seen
seen_at

or equivalent persistence.
