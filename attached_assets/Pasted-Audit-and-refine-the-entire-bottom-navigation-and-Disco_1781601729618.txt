Audit and refine the entire bottom navigation and Discover page layout for premium mobile UX.

Current observations:

1. The bottom navigation feels visually unbalanced.
- Likes appears naturally centred and draws the eye more than the other tabs.
- Discover feels left-heavy.
- Connections feels crowded because of the longer label.
- Profile feels visually smaller than the other icons.
- Overall spacing and visual weight are inconsistent.

Required improvements:

BOTTOM NAVIGATION

- Give every navigation item identical width.
- Centre icons independently from text length.
- Ensure all icons are perfectly aligned on the same baseline.
- Increase icon size by approximately 10%.
- Reduce label size slightly for a cleaner premium appearance.
- Increase spacing between icons and labels.
- Ensure Discover, Intent, Likes, Connections and Profile all have equal visual weight.
- Active tab should remain rose coloured.
- Inactive tabs should use a softer neutral colour.
- Verify perfect alignment on iPhone Safari, Chrome and desktop.

DISCOVER PAGE

The profile photo currently dominates too much vertical space.

Required:

- Reduce mobile photo height by approximately 10–15%.
- Keep photo width unchanged.
- Preserve carousel behaviour.
- Preserve Open / Close actions.
- Preserve rounded corners.
- Preserve photo quality.

Use the newly gained space to:

- Show more conversation starters.
- Show more profile personality information above the fold.
- Reduce the amount of scrolling required.
- Improve overall balance between photo and profile content.

VALIDATION

Test on:
- iPhone Safari
- iPhone Chrome
- Desktop Chrome
- Desktop Safari

Confirm:
- Bottom navigation is visually balanced.
- No icons shift position.
- Active tab remains clear.
- Discover page feels less photo-heavy.
- More profile information is visible without scrolling.
