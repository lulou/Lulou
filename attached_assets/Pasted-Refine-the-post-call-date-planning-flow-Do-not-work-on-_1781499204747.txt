Keep Messaging Behaviour
Choose and implement the best behaviour:

Option A — Unlimited after choosing Keep Messaging:

Users can keep chatting freely.
Plan a Date button remains available in chat.
They can plan whenever ready.
Option B — Additional limited messaging block:

Give another limited amount of messages each.
After the extra limit, show Plan a Date again.
Recommended:
Use Option A unless there is a strong technical or product reason not to.

Reason:
If both users have completed 15 messages, a call, and 25 more messages each, they have earned enough trust. At that point, the app should strongly encourage a date but not trap them.
