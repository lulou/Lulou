1. VOICE NOTES (HIGH PRIORITY)

Voice notes currently feel broken.

Current issues:

• Holding the microphone doesn't consistently begin recording.
• Sometimes the message composer collapses while trying to record.
• Recording unexpectedly stops.
• Voice notes don't always send correctly.
• Hold-to-record behaviour feels unreliable.
• The interaction is not smooth like iMessage or WhatsApp.

Required behaviour:

• First use requests microphone permission once only.
• After permission is granted, never ask again unless the OS requires it.
• User presses and HOLDS the microphone.
• Recording starts instantly.
• Recording continues while finger remains down.
• Releasing the finger immediately sends the voice note automatically.
• No separate Send button.
• No extra confirmation.
• No collapsing message bar.
• No accidental cancellation.
• No interrupted recordings.
• Smooth waveform animation while recording.
• Recording timer updates live.
• Haptic feedback when recording starts and sends.
• If recording is less than 0.5 seconds, cancel instead of sending.

Audit every touch event:

pointerdown
pointerup
pointercancel
touchstart
touchend
touchcancel

Ensure iOS Safari/PWA behaves correctly.
