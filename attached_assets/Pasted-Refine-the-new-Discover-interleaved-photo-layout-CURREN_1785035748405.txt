Refine the new Discover interleaved-photo layout.

CURRENT PROBLEM

The photos are now distributed through the profile, but too many written response sections are grouped together in the middle.

In the current production layout:

- personality
- conversation starters
- “They’d love to know”
- “Ask me”

appear one after another before the next photo.

The final photo is then followed by very little written information.

This still makes the profile feel unbalanced.

GOAL

Distribute both the uploaded photos and the written profile sections across the full height of the profile.

There must be meaningful profile content:

- before the middle photos
- between the middle photos
- after the final uploaded photo

The final photo must not automatically become the last major item in the profile.

Some written sections should appear beneath the final photo so the profile continues naturally and ends with useful information rather than ending abruptly on an image.

DYNAMIC CONTENT GROUPING

Do not render every written section as one flat block.

Build all available profile sections as an ordered list, including only sections that contain data:

- basic details
- personality traits
- conversation starters
- “They’d love to know”
- “Ask me”
- looking for
- green flags
- connection pace
- Connection DNA signals
- compatibility insights
- remaining profile questions
- final actions or closing profile information

Then divide those written sections into balanced content groups based on:

- number of available sections
- number of uploaded photos
- amount of content inside each section

Interleave the content groups and photos so the complete profile feels evenly paced.

IMPORTANT FINAL-PHOTO RULE

Always reserve at least one meaningful written content group for after the final inserted photo whenever enough profile content exists.

For example, with six photos and a complete profile:

1. Hero photo
2. Basic details and personality
3. Photo 2
4. Conversation starters
5. Photo 3
6. “They’d love to know”
7. Photo 4
8. “Ask me”
9. Photo 5
10. Looking for and green flags
11. Photo 6
12. Connection pace
13. Connection DNA signals or compatibility information
14. Final profile information/actions

Do not hardcode this exact order.

Calculate the distribution dynamically from the sections that actually contain content.

Profiles with missing answers must still remain balanced.

CONTENT WEIGHTING

Do not treat every section as the same size.

Estimate section weight using the amount of content:

- basic details: small
- personality chips: small
- looking for: small
- pace: small
- green flags: medium
- one response card: medium
- multiple conversation starters: large
- multiple “Ask me” cards: large
- DNA/compatibility section: medium or large

Use these weights to avoid placing several large response sections together.

The algorithm should spread larger sections across different photo intervals.

Do not place:

- conversation starters
- “They’d love to know”
- “Ask me”

all consecutively unless there are too few photos or no other content sections.

BALANCED LAYOUT TARGET

For profiles with enough content:

- each photo should have written information near it
- no area should contain several long response sections without a visual break
- no photo should be immediately followed by another photo
- the final photo should normally have profile content beneath it
- the ending should feel intentional, not empty

PHOTO COUNTS

1 photo:
- hero followed by all written sections

2 photos:
- hero
- roughly half the written content
- second photo
- remaining written content

3 photos:
- hero
- first content group
- photo 2
- second content group
- photo 3
- final content group

4–6 photos:
- distribute photos and content groups across the full profile
- reserve content beneath the final photo
- avoid tiny single-line groups between large images

PRESERVE THE EXISTING SWIPE/PULL

This refinement must not change the current horizontal swipe/pull system.

Non-negotiable:

- do not modify the outer swipe wrapper
- do not modify swipe thresholds
- do not modify resistance, rotation, snap-back or dismiss animations
- do not modify left/right swipe actions
- do not add gesture handlers to photos or content groups
- inserted content remains ordinary vertical content
- vertical scrolling must not accidentally trigger horizontal movement
- horizontal swiping must still work when starting directly over any inserted photo

The swipe behaviour must remain exactly as it is now.
