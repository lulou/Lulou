Fix signup/session leakage between accounts.

Current bug:
I tried to sign up with a new test email using verify+test.
The app said "email sent".
I went to my email and the email was not there.
When I returned to the app, I was somehow signed into my normal existing account instead of staying on the verify-email screen.

This must never happen.

Expected behaviour:
After signup with a new email:
- If email verification is required, the user should NOT be logged in.
- The app should show the "Check your email" / verify-email screen.
- The app must NOT restore a previous account session.
- The app must NOT automatically switch to another existing user.
- The app must NOT enter the app until the new email is verified.

Audit and fix:

1. Before starting signup, explicitly clear any existing Supabase session.
2. After signup returns user + session:null, stay locked on the verify-email screen.
3. Do not call getSession and let an old session restore the previous account.
4. Store pending signup email separately, e.g. pendingVerificationEmail.
5. If there is a pending signup email, AppContent must not allow a different existing session to enter the app.
6. If Supabase still has an old session in localStorage, clear it during signup.
7. On app resume/focus after signup, do not restore the previous account.
8. Only allow entry after the verification callback signs in the newly verified email.
9. If the user manually presses Back, show:
  "Finish verifying your email to continue."
  with options:
  - Resend email
  - Use a different email
  - Sign in instead
10. Add logs:
  [VERIFY] SIGNUP_START_CLEAR_SESSION
  [VERIFY] PENDING_EMAIL_SET
  [VERIFY] OLD_SESSION_BLOCKED
  [VERIFY] SESSION_EMAIL_MISMATCH_BLOCKED
  [VERIFY] VERIFIED_EMAIL_MATCHED

Also audit why the email did not arrive:
- Confirm Supabase signUp returned no error.
- Log rate limit errors.
- Log email already registered errors.
- If the email already exists, show a clear message instead of pretending a new verification was sent.
- Check spam/junk wording is fine, but do not rely on that only.

Validation:
Test with two accounts:
1. Be logged into existing Account A.
2. Start signup for Account B.
3. App must clear Account A session.
4. App shows verification screen for Account B.
5. Leaving and returning to the app must not restore Account A.
6. Account B cannot enter app until email verified.
7. After clicking verification email, Account B enters onboarding/app.
8. Account A is not restored accidentally.

Run:
npm run check
npm run build

Report:
- Root cause
- Files changed
- How old sessions are blocked
- How pending verification email is stored
- How email-not-received cases are handled
