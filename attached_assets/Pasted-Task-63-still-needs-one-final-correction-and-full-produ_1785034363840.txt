CURRENT GAP

POST /api/matches/:matchId/messages returns progressionEvent only to the client that sends the threshold-crossing request.

At 8 messages each way, the second user to send their eighth message receives the immediate voice-note event. The first user may not receive their celebration until the 60-second entitlement poll runs.

- polling may remain only as recovery

- voice-note milestone pending/acknowledged
- first-call milestone pending/acknowledged
