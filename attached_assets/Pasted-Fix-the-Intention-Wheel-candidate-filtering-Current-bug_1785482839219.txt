Fix the Intention Wheel candidate filtering.

Current bug:
The user has gone through everyone in Discover, and now the Intention page shows “No profiles to show yet.”

This is wrong.

Discover and Intention are separate experiences. A profile being viewed, passed, closed or exhausted in Discover must not automatically remove that profile from the Intention Wheel.

Required behaviour:

1. The Intention Wheel must use its own eligibility history.
2. Do not exclude candidates merely because the current user:
  - viewed them in Discover
  - passed them in Discover
  - closed them in Discover
  - exhausted the Discover feed
3. Only exclude a profile from Intention when appropriate, such as:
  - blocked
  - paused
  - deleted
  - incompatible gender/preference
  - active match already exists
  - already acted on through the Intention Wheel itself
  - any genuine safety or account restriction
4. Keep separate records for:
  - Discover interactions
  - Intention Wheel interactions
5. A Discover close/pass must not count as an Intention close.
6. If eligible profiles exist, the wheel must show them even when Discover is empty.
7. Do not convert API errors into a genuine empty state.
8. “No profiles to show yet” must only appear after a successful response with zero truly eligible Intention candidates.

Investigate the current filter chain for /api/popular or the Intention candidate query.

Look specifically for shared exclusions based on:
- all interactions
- Discover close rows
- shownIds
- previouslySeenIds
- dismissed profiles
- accumulated Discover history

Remove those Discover-only exclusions from the Intention query.

Test:

1. Create two compatible accounts.
2. View and pass Account B in Discover from Account A.
3. Exhaust Account A’s Discover feed.
4. Open Intent.
5. Confirm Account B can still appear in the Intention Wheel.
6. Act on Account B through Intent.
7. Confirm only then is Account B excluded according to Intention rules.
8. Refresh and reopen.
9. Confirm the wheel remains independent from Discover history.

Do not change Discover behaviour.
