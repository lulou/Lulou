Build the final production-quality Call Stability & Audio pass for Lulou.

Calls are now much better than before, but they still are not at the level of FaceTime, WhatsApp or Messenger.

This is now a quality and reliability task, not a feature task.

Do not redesign the call UI.
Do not break the existing call flow.
Focus only on making calls feel stable, clear and premium.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OBJECTIVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Two matched users should be able to start an audio or video call and experience:

• Clear voices
• No screeching
• No beeping
• No echo
• Fast connection
• Stable audio
• Smooth reconnects
• Natural speaker behaviour
• Premium experience

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. FULL WEBRTC AUDIT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Audit the complete call pipeline:

• signalling
• SDP exchange
• ICE gathering
• STUN
• TURN
• relay usage
• ICE restarts
• candidate selection
• connection state
• reconnect logic
• duplicate peer connections
• duplicate audio tracks
• duplicate HTMLAudioElements
• duplicate MediaStreams
• audio routing
• cleanup after call

Eliminate every duplicate path.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. AUDIO QUALITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Calls should sound like normal phone calls.

Investigate:

• screeching
• echo
• ringing bleeding into microphone
• duplicate playback
• remote audio looping
• local audio leakage
• feedback
• clipping
• gain spikes
• incorrect sink routing
• race conditions during connection

Ensure:

• echoCancellation
• noiseSuppression
• autoGainControl

remain enabled throughout the entire call lifecycle.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. CONNECTION SPEED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Optimise:

• call setup
• ICE negotiation
• TURN selection
• reconnect speed

Reduce unnecessary waits.

Pre-create anything safe to pre-create.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. SPEAKER / AUDIO ROUTING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Desired behaviour:

Audio call:
• default = phone/earpiece where supported
• Speaker button switches to loudspeaker
• Toggle instantly
• Never unexpectedly jump to loudspeaker

Video call:
• default = loudspeaker
• Speaker toggle still works

Respect platform limitations.

Do not fake unsupported behaviour.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. RINGTONE / CONNECT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Ensure:

• ringtone stops immediately once connected
• no ringtone audio leaks into microphone
• no leftover audio elements
• no ringing after answer
• no beeps during transition

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
6. NETWORK RESILIENCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Handle gracefully:

• Wi-Fi → mobile
• mobile → Wi-Fi
• brief packet loss
• temporary ICE disconnect
• background → foreground
• app interruption

Reconnect without forcing users to redial whenever possible.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
7. PERFORMANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Reduce:

• unnecessary re-renders
• duplicate streams
• duplicate peer connections
• unnecessary renegotiations
• unnecessary getUserMedia calls

Keep CPU and battery usage low.

8. USER EXPERIENCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Calls should feel premium.

Animations:

• smooth connect
• smooth disconnect
• smooth mute
• smooth speaker toggle
• smooth camera switch

No flashing.

No jumping UI.
