PART 1 – Simplify Membership

Proposed Membership:

$14.99/month

Membership should remove friction.

Membership includes:

Priority Discovery
1 Undo Last Pass per month
1 Undo Last Unmatch per month
1 Phone Call Credit per month
Future Voice Notes Unlock access (if included later)

Membership should NOT replace call packs.

PART 3 – +5 Messages Rework

Current issue:

+5 Messages feels disconnected from the user journey.

Lulou journey:

Discovery
→ Match
→ Chat
→ Phone Call
→ Meet

Many users will reach the call stage but not feel comfortable enough yet.

Purpose of message packs:

“I like this person but I’m not ready for a phone call.”

Review:

+5 Messages
+10 Messages
Message bundles

and recommend the best approach.

Important:

Chat eventually becomes unlimited.

Message purchases should only help users extend conversation before reaching that stage.

⸻

PART 4 – Voice Notes Unlock

Future feature.

One-time purchase.

Permanent access.

Not a subscription.

Not pay-per-message.

Not tied to specific matches.

If purchased:

Voice note icon becomes active.
Voice notes available across all current and future matches.

Conversation rule:

If one user owns Voice Notes Unlock:

Both users in that conversation can exchange voice notes.

Reason:

Voice notes are a conversation feature.

One-way voice messaging creates a poor experience.

Review:

pricing
storage
moderation
duration limits
upload limits
UX

before implementation.

Pricing:

1 free Undo Last Unmatch per month

Non-members:

$2.99

Important:

Review whether:
